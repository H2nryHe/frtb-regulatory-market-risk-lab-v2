## Comment Period

The Federal Reserve proposal page and Federal Register publication both record
the comment deadline as 2026-06-18. Expiration of that comment deadline is not a
final rule and is not treated as an effective date.

## Final Rule Check

The Phase 9 status check used official Federal Reserve, Federal Register, OCC
and FDIC sources. No official R-1887 final rule, withdrawal, or supplemental
revised proposal was located during the Phase 9 retrieval. The live Federal
Reserve R-1887 page still classifies the item as a Rulemaking Proposal.

## Project Treatment

Phase 9 is regulatory analysis, crosswalk and gap analysis only. It does not
replace the Basel source basis for Phases 0-8, does not load U.S. proposal
parameters into Basel configs, and does not create an operational U.S.
calculation engine.

## Claim Boundary

The U.S. material is analyzed as proposed rulemaking unless official current
sources state otherwise. This project must not imply that the comment deadline
converted the proposal into a final rule.

The existing calculation engine remains a selected Basel market-risk mechanics
lab. It must not be presented as current U.S. regulatory compliance, as a final
U.S. rules implementation, or as a U.S. proposal portfolio capital result.
